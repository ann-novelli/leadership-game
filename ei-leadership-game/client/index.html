Five Components of Emotional Intelligence

🧠 Self-Awareness

Understanding your emotions, strengths, weaknesses, and their impact on others

⚖️ Self-Regulation

Controlling disruptive impulses and adapting to change

🎯 Motivation

Passion for work beyond money or status; drive to achieve

❤️ Empathy

Understanding the emotional makeup of other people

🤝 Social Skills

Managing relationships and building networks effectively

Welcome to the EI Leadership Game

About This Game

You'll face workplace leadership scenarios and choose how you would respond. Each choice reflects different aspects of emotional intelligence.

How It Works

  1. Read each scenario carefully (you'll have 90 seconds)
  2. Select the response that best matches your approach
  3. After everyone responds, we'll discuss the choices together
  4. At the end, you'll see your EI profile and areas for growth

Remember

  • There are no "perfect" answers - this is about self-awareness
  • Be honest in your responses
  • Participate actively in discussions
  • Learn from different perspectives
